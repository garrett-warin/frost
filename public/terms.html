01 Accounts

Use an email account you control when creating or signing in to your Grove account. Only submit verification codes you generated yourself. Do not share your verification codes or account access with other people.

Do not access someone else’s account or create another account to get around a suspension, ban or required username change.

02 Username policies

Nicknames and made-up names are welcome. Your username does not have to identify you or include your real name.

  • No spam or advertising. No repetitive spam, promotions, referral codes or contact requests. A playful name is fine.
  • No impersonation. Do not pretend to be another person, an administrator or an official Grove account, including through misleading lookalike names.
  • No harassment, hate or threats. No slurs, targeted insults, promotion of hateful groups or threats of harm.
  • Keep it appropriate. No sexually explicit names or graphic violence. Do not disguise prohibited words with extra letters, numbers or symbols.
  • No sensitive personal details. Do not include phone numbers, home addresses, passwords or someone else’s private information.
  • Follow the format. User-chosen names must be available and use 3–24 letters, numbers, underscores or dashes. Administrator-assigned reset names may use a longer account identifier.

03 Browsing

Visiting poki.com or crazygames.com through Grove is not allowed.

04 Conduct within Grove

Do not spam support tickets, harass users or administrators, submit deliberately false reports, or disrupt Grove’s services. Do not attempt to gain unauthorized access to accounts or administrator controls.

05 Moderation & appeals

Administrators may reset a username, require a username change, suspend an account or ban an account when these rules are broken. The action may depend on the seriousness and repetition of the violation.

If you think an action was taken by mistake, use Support & appeals in your Grove account. Explain what happened and include relevant details. An appeal does not guarantee that an action will be reversed.

06 Availability

Grove may be unavailable during maintenance, outages or updates. Features and available links may change. Access to every website, uninterrupted service and compatibility with every device are not guaranteed.

07 Changes to these terms

Updates will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be announced in Grove before taking effect.

08 Contact

For account help or appeals, use your account’s Support & appeals page. For other questions, email hi@garretts.space.
